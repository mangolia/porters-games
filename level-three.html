Which weapon?

The spear arcs and has to be charged — that's the aiming skill your whole game is built on. The machine gun sprays. It feels great and it makes aiming almost free, so the tank gets a lot tougher to keep it interesting.

Want the whole army?

100 guys plus the tank. That's 118 spears that all have to hit, so this button also gives you 170 spears, 25 health, and a much faster backflip. It is almost certainly unfair. That's the point — go find out what breaks.

Porter's block move

Hit an orange shell in mid-air and it pops — and your spear carries straight on through, so it can still hit the tank or a soldier behind it. One throw can block and damage. That was Porter's idea and it's the best thing in the game.

Note: blocking works on shells. The soldiers' blue-tipped spears go past — backflip those.
