Two bugs fixed — both found by Porter

Bug 1: his spears always sailed underneath you. He was aiming straight at you, and gravity pulled the spear down 92 pixels during the flight — so it arrived 52 pixels too low, every single throw. Perfect aim at the wrong place.

Bug 2: he lobbed spears almost straight up. The first fix worked by guessing and then improving the guess. On easy throws that's fine. On hard ones the guess ran away upward — at high gravity he was throwing at 88°, nearly vertical. Now the angle is solved exactly instead of guessed.

Set His aim to Deadly and he will genuinely hit you. Now the backflip is worth testing.

About the backflip cooldown

Porter hasn't picked what the flip costs yet, so it's a 1.2s cooldown placeholder — a guess, not a decision.

Drag Flip cooldown to 0 and you can flip forever. Try it on purpose, notice how fast the level stops being interesting, then find the number where it's actually fun.
